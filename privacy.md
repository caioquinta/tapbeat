# Tapbeat privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Tapbeat is a rhythm game made by Caio Teixeira da Quinta. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how to turn
it off. Questions or requests: [ruivocanhotogames@gmail.com](mailto:ruivocanhotogames@gmail.com).

## In short

- No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.
- Gameplay data is only sent if you agree. The game asks the first time you open it, and you can change your answer
  any time in **Settings → Gameplay data**.
- Your scores, settings and audio calibration stay on your device.
- We never sell your data.

## What stays on your device

High scores and best grades, volume and vibration settings, audio calibration, your sharing choice and how many times you have played
each song. This is stored only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the game.

## What is sent if you agree to share gameplay data

We use [Unity Analytics](https://unity.com/products/analytics) (Unity Technologies) to understand how the game is
played and where it needs improving:

- **How you play:** when you open the game and How to Play, which songs you start, finish, pause, retry or leave, how
  far into a song you got, your score, grade, accuracy and hit counts, and audio calibration results.
- **Your ratings and feedback:** the optional "how fun / how did it feel" rating after a song, and any text you write in
  **Send feedback**. Please don't include personal information in feedback.
- **Device and app information:** device model, operating system, app version, language, the type of audio output in
  use (speaker, Bluetooth or wired, never the device's name), and your approximate country, which Unity works out from
  your IP address.
- **An anonymous identifier** created by the game, so a session's events can be grouped. It is not linked to your
  name, email or phone number.

## Crash reports

If the game crashes, Unity Cloud Diagnostics sends a crash report (what went wrong in the code, device model,
operating system and app version) so we can fix it. Reports of errors inside the game are only sent if you agreed to
share gameplay data; reports of full crashes of the app are always sent and contain no personal information.

## Why we use it

Only to improve Tapbeat: find songs that are too hard or too easy, spots where players give up, and bugs. We don't use
it for advertising and don't combine it with other data about you.

## Who receives it

Unity Technologies processes the data on our behalf, under its own
[privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). Nobody else receives it. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS).

## How long it's kept

Unity keeps analytics data for a limited time under its data retention rules; we only look at aggregated reports.

## Turning it off and deleting your data

Turn **Settings → Gameplay data** to **OFF**. The game stops sending gameplay data and asks Unity to delete the data
already collected from your device. You can also email [ruivocanhotogames@gmail.com](mailto:ruivocanhotogames@gmail.com) to ask for deletion.

## Children

Tapbeat is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
